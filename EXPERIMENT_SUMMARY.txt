KEY FINDINGS: SPECTROGRAM DIMENSION IMPACT
================================================================================

MEL BINS COMPARISON (MobileNetV3Small, same architecture):
  • 48 mel bins (48x300): 79.00% INT8 accuracy  ❌ -15% from optimal
  • 64 mel bins (64x300): 93-95% INT8 accuracy  ✅ OPTIMAL (SWEET SPOT)
  • 80 mel bins (80x300): 82.00% INT8 accuracy  ❌ -12% from optimal

CONCLUSION: 64 mel bins is the OPTIMAL frequency resolution for seabird classification.
            Both reducing (48) and increasing (80) mel bins causes significant accuracy loss.
            This indicates the model optimally utilizes frequency information at 64 bins.
            64x300 spectrograms provide the ideal balance for this task.

WIDTH SCALING COMPARISON (MobileNetV3Small, 64x300 spectrograms):
  • Full width (1.0x):  93-95% INT8, 1995.8 KB  ✅ BEST ACCURACY
  • 0.75x width:        90% INT8, 1268.6 KB     ✅ SIZE/ACCURACY TRADEOFF

DROPOUT IMPACT (MobileNetV3Small width=0.75, 64x300, no augmentation):
  • Dropout 0.2:        90.33% INT8 (rand100)  ✅ OPTIMAL
  • Dropout 0.0:        82.22% INT8 (rand42)   ❌ -8% degradation

CONCLUSION: Dropout is ESSENTIAL for the width-scaled model (0.75x).
            Removing dropout causes 8% accuracy loss, suggesting the narrower
            model benefits from this regularization to prevent overfitting.

AUGMENTATION IMPACT:
  • No augmentation:    92.89-94.56% (best with seed 786)
  • Mixup (α=0.2):      93.22-93.67% (minimal improvement)
  • Conclusion: Augmentation NOT required for 90%+ accuracy
